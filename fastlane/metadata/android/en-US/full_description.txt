Vynody is a visually refined, feature-rich, and lightweight local music player built with Flutter and native audio backends.

Key Features:
- Fast local media library indexing with low system resource usage
- Broad audio format support powered by Media3 (ExoPlayer) and FFmpeg
- Karaoke-style word-by-word synced lyrics
- Serverless cross-device LAN syncing for music tracks, playlists, and lyrics
- WebDAV, SMB, Subsonic, and Jellyfin network storage playback support
- Customizable themes and progress bar visual styles

Network Usage:
Local playback works completely offline. Network permissions and cleartext traffic are used for local LAN device discovery (mDNS) and peer-to-peer sync/remote control, connecting to user-configured self-hosted servers (WebDAV, SMB via a local loopback proxy, Subsonic, Jellyfin) and custom stream URLs, as well as optional online metadata/lyrics lookup (LRCLIB, MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, AcoustID, TheAudioDB), Last.fm scrobbling, and user-configured AI lyrics services.
